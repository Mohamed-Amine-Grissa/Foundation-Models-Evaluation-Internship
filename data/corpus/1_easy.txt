The little dog ran across the yard. The sun was warm and bright. The dog saw a red ball near the tree. He picked up the ball with his mouth. He ran back to the house. The little girl smiled and clapped her hands. She threw the ball again. The dog ran fast and caught it. They played in the yard all afternoon. The birds sang in the trees above them. When the sun began to set, the girl called the dog inside. The dog followed her happily. They both had dinner together. Then the girl read a short story before bed. The dog slept at the foot of her bed. Outside, the stars came out one by one. The night was calm and quiet. In the morning, the sun rose again. The dog woke up first and waited by the door. The girl got dressed and ate breakfast. Then they went back outside to play. The ball was still lying in the grass where they left it. The dog ran to get it right away. The girl laughed and ran after him. It was going to be another happy day for both of them. Every morning the sun came up over the hill behind their house. The dog would wake first and wait quietly by the door until someone let him out. He liked to walk slowly around the yard, checking every corner before running to find his ball. The girl often watched from her window while she got dressed for the day. Once she came downstairs, they would eat breakfast together at the small kitchen table. Her mother packed a simple lunch while the dog waited nearby, hoping for a small piece of toast. After breakfast, they always went back outside to play before the day grew too warm.
